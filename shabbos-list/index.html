The list that runs itself

Friday afternoon shouldn’t depend on someone remembering. The list knows which week it is and who is doing what.

  • Resets after ShabbosThe list clears itself on Motzei Shabbos, ready for next week.
  • Assign tasksGive each job to a family member so nothing is done twice or forgotten.
  • Group tasksKitchen, table, lights, timers: keep related jobs together.
  • Only when neededTasks that show up only for some weeks, like guests or a Yom Tov that follows Shabbos.
  • Reusable templatesStart from a ready-made list and change it to fit your home.
