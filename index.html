Halachic Alarm Clock

Beta · version 1.1.1 Android 5.0 and up English · עברית

Set an alarm for “15 minutes before candle lighting” or “at sunrise” once, and it rings at the right time every day, wherever you are. The app shows the day’s zmanim, the Hebrew date and what’s coming up, and stays quiet on Shabbos and Yom Tov unless you tell it otherwise.

Alarms by zman

Pick any zman, then minutes or hours before or after it. Repeat on chosen days, add a label, choose the sound. The alarm recalculates every day.

Quiet on Shabbos and Yom Tov

Alarms skip Shabbos and Yom Tov automatically, with one day in Israel and two outside. Turn ringing back on for any single alarm, or add erev Yom Tov to a Friday alarm.

Your shitos, your minhag

Choose the opinions you follow (MGA or GRA, dawn and nightfall, candle lighting minutes, when Shabbos ends). Tap any zman to see how it is calculated. Compare with my luach finds which opinion your printed calendar uses.

Hebrew calendar

  • Hebrew date, parsha, holidays, fasts and Rosh Chodesh
  • Erev Pesach chametz times
  • Daf Yomi and other daily learning
  • Yahrzeits and Hebrew birthdays, each year on the right date

Reminders

  • Sefiras HaOmer, in Hebrew and English
  • Chanukah candles, Kiddush Levana, Eruv Tavshilin
  • Tal U’matar and Birkas Ha’ilanos
  • Optional Do Not Disturb for Shabbos

Built to ring

Alarms use Android’s exact alarm clock, so they ring even when the app is closed. An Alarm check finds settings that could stop an alarm and fixes them. When you travel, times follow your location. No account, no ads, and your data stays on your phone.

How to install

Install on your Android phone

Do these steps on the phone itself. Button names vary a little between phone makers and Android versions.

  1. Download the file

    Open this page in Chrome (or your usual browser) on your phone and tap Download APK. If the browser warns that “this type of file can harm your device,” tap Download anyway.

  2. Open it

    When the download finishes, tap Open in the notification or at the bottom of the browser. You can also find it later in the Files app under Downloads.

  3. Allow installs from your browser

    Android asks for permission the first time you install an app from outside the Play Store. Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then press back.

    You only need to do this once. You can turn it off again after installing.

  4. Install

    Tap Install. Google Play Protect may say it doesn’t recognize the developer. That is expected for apps not yet on the Play Store: tap More details, then Install anyway.

  5. Open the app and finish setup

    A short setup asks for your location and minhag. Then allow what the alarms need to ring on time:

    • Notifications, so the alarm can show on screen.
    • Alarms & reminders, so it rings at the exact minute.
    • Location (optional), for accurate zmanim. You can pick a city instead.

    Then open Settings › Alarm check. It finds anything that could stop an alarm, such as battery saving, and fixes it with one tap.

Updating to a new version

Download the new APK from this page and install it the same way. Your alarms and settings are kept.

“App not installed”

This usually means an older copy from a different source is already on the phone. Uninstall it first, then install again.

An alarm didn’t ring

Run Settings › Alarm check first. Then open Settings › Alarm history and tap Send report, so we can see what happened.
