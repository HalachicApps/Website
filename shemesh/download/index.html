How to join

Three steps to the beta

Use the same Google account for every step: the one you use on your Android phone.

  1. Join the testers group

    Open the group and tap Join group (or Ask to join). If it asks for approval, we’ll let you in soon, and you’ll get a welcome email with these same steps.

    Open the testers group

  2. Become a tester

    On your Android phone, open the testing page and tap Become a tester. This only works once you’re in the group.

    Open the testing page

  3. Install from Google Play

    Tap the link on the testing page, or open Shemesh in the Play Store and tap Install. It can take a few minutes to appear after you opt in.

    Open in Google Play

  4. Open the app and finish setup

    A short setup asks for your location and minhag. Then allow what the alarms need to ring on time:

    • Notifications, so the alarm can show on screen.
    • Alarms & reminders, so it rings at the exact minute.
    • Location (optional), for accurate zmanim. You can pick a city instead.

    Then open Settings › Alarm check. It finds anything that could stop an alarm, such as battery saving, and fixes it with one tap.

Updates

New beta versions install through Google Play like any other app update. Your alarms and settings are kept.

No Install button?

Check that you joined the group and opted in with the same Google account you use in the Play Store, then wait a few minutes and refresh.

An alarm didn’t ring

Run Settings › Alarm check first. Then open Settings › Alarm history and tap Send report, so we can see what happened.
