Shemesh – Zmanim Alarm Clock

Privacy policy

Shemesh keeps your information on your phone. Last updated October 19, 2026.

Stays on your phone

Your location, alarms and settings are never sent to us or to anyone else. The app doesn’t connect to the internet by itself.

Nothing to track you

No accounts, no ads and no analytics.

Payments by Google

Pro is bought through Google Play. We never see your name or payment details.

Who we are

Shemesh – Zmanim Alarm Clock is made by Halachic Applications LLC. Questions about this policy: shemeshalarmclock@gmail.com.

What the app uses, and why

Location, when you allow itTo work out sunrise, sunset and the other zmanim where you are.
Location in the background, only if you turn on “Follow me in the background”Shemesh then collects your location in the background, even when the app is closed or not in use, to move your alarms when you travel. It uses only positions the phone already has, plus one about every half hour. It is off by default, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings or in your phone’s permissions.
Alarms, settings, saved places, yahrzeits and birthdaysStored only on your phone, so the app can work.
Notifications, exact alarms, full-screen alerts, Do Not DisturbSo alarms ring on time and, if you choose, stay quiet on Shabbos and Yom Tov.

Your location is never sent anywhere or shared.

Backups and exports

  • Android Backup. Android may back up the app’s data to your own Google account. That is between you and Google, and you can switch it off in your phone’s settings.
  • Export. “Export” in Settings saves a file that you choose where to keep. It contains your settings, which include your location, so take care when sharing it.

Shemesh Pro and payments

Pro is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment and tells the app only whether Pro was bought. We don’t see your name, card or any payment details. Pro can also be unlocked with a Pro code, which is checked on your phone.

Feedback and diagnostics

If you choose to send feedback or a diagnostics report, the app opens your email app with a message for you to read and send. Nothing is sent unless you press send.

Children

Shemesh is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with a new date.
