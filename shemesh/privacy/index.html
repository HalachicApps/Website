Shemesh privacy policy

Last updated October 19, 2026. Shemesh – Zmanim Alarm Clock is made by Halachic Applications LLC.

The short version

Shemesh keeps your information on your phone. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it does not send your location, alarms or settings to us or to anyone else. The app does not connect to the internet by itself.

What the app uses, and why

  • Location (when you allow it): to work out sunrise, sunset and the other zmanim where you are. If you turn on "Follow me in the background", Shemesh also collects your location in the background, even when the app is closed or not in use, to move your alarms when you travel. It only uses positions the phone already has, plus one about every half hour. Locations stay on your phone and are never sent or shared. You can turn this off at any time in Settings, or take back the permission in your phone's settings.
  • Your alarms, settings, saved places, yahrzeits and birthdays: stored only on your phone, so the app can work.
  • Notifications, exact alarms, full-screen alerts and Do Not Disturb: so alarms ring on time and, if you choose, stay quiet on Shabbos and Yom Tov.

Backups and exports

  • Android may back up the app's data to your own Google account (Android Backup). That is between you and Google, and you can switch it off in your phone's settings.
  • "Export" in Settings saves a file that you choose where to keep. It contains your settings, which include your location, so take care when sharing it.

Shemesh Pro and payments

Pro is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment and tells the app only whether Pro was bought. We do not see your name, card or any payment details. Pro can also be unlocked with a Pro code, which is checked on your phone.

Feedback and diagnostics

If you choose to send feedback or a diagnostics report, the app opens your email app with a message for you to read and send. Nothing is sent unless you press send.

Children

Shemesh is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with a new date.
