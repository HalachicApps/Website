Shemesh – Zmanim Alarm Clock
Beta · version 1.6.0 Android 5.0 and up English · עברית

Your phone, set to zmanim.

Set an alarm for “15 minutes before candle lighting” or “at sunrise” once, and it rings at the right time every day, wherever you are. It stays quiet on Shabbos and Yom Tov unless you tell it otherwise.

Features

Everything the day needs

Everything in this section is free, and always will be.

Alarms by zman

Pick any zman, then minutes or hours before or after it. The editor says the alarm back to you in words (“18 min before sunset · Every Friday”) and shows the next times it will ring. Start from ready-made davening alarms if you like.

Rules for real life

  • Only on, or never on, fast days, Rosh Chodesh, erev Shabbos, Chol HaMoed, Elul and more
  • Only between two dates each year, Hebrew or English
  • “No later than” or “not before” a set time
  • Groups you turn on or off together, like a summer schedule

Skip, pause and undo

Skip just the next ring, or pause every alarm until a date and they come back by themselves. Duplicate an alarm, and undo a delete. Each alarm can have its own sound and volume, vibrate only, or vibrate first and then ring.

Quiet on Shabbos and Yom Tov

Alarms skip Shabbos and Yom Tov automatically, with one day in Israel and two outside. Turn ringing back on for any single alarm, or use a Shabbos alarm that rings briefly and stops by itself. Optional Do Not Disturb until Shabbos ends.

Today at a glance

The next alarm, the zmanim coming up, what changes in davening today, special Shabbosos, the molad and Kiddush Levana, the parsha and Daf Yomi. Choose what shows and in what order.

Your shitos, your minhag

Choose the opinions you follow (MGA or GRA, dawn and nightfall, candle lighting minutes, when Shabbos ends), with Ashkenaz, Sephardi, Israeli, Chabad and Chassidish customs. Tap any zman to see how it is worked out, and Compare with my luach finds which opinion your printed calendar uses.

Hebrew calendar and learning

  • Hebrew date, parsha, holidays, fasts and Rosh Chodesh
  • Daf Yomi, Mishnah Yomis, Rambam, Tehillim, Chitas and more
  • Yahrzeits and Hebrew birthdays, each year on the right date
  • Share a day’s zmanim as a picture

Reminders

  • Sefiras HaOmer, in Hebrew and English
  • Chanukah, Kiddush Levana, Eruv Tavshilin, Tal U’matar
  • Eat before a fast, Elul, Selichos, chametz, lulav, Purim, the Seder
  • A daily learning time. Every kind can be turned off

Home-screen widgets

The next zman with a countdown, time left until Shabbos, Yom Tov or a fast, the Hebrew date, your next alarm with Skip, today’s zmanim, tonight’s Omer count and Chanukah candles, daily learning, coming yahrzeits and birthdays, and this Shabbos. Each in navy, your wallpaper colors or see-through.

Tools

  • Date converter, Hebrew and English, before or after sunset
  • Mezuzah and tefillin checks, with a reminder when one is due
  • Tefillas HaDerech, with a reminder when you leave town

Phones and tablets

On a tablet, or a phone turned sideways, Today and Zmanim spread into two columns and the tabs move to the side. On a phone it stays simple.

Built to ring, and yours

Alarms use Android’s exact alarm clock, so they ring even when the app is closed. An Alarm check finds settings that could stop an alarm and fixes them. Backup & transfer moves everything to a new phone. No account, no ads, no internet access: your data stays on your phone.

Shemesh Pro

Extras for those who want more

Pro adds tools and extras on top of the free app. Alarms, zmanim and every mitzvah reminder are never part of Pro.

Tools

  • Bar and bat mitzvah date, with its parsha
  • Year summary: earliest and latest Shabbos, fast hours, longest day
  • Year chart of dawn, sunrise, sunset and nightfall
  • Minyan schedule for a shul (see below)

Dashboard

A full-screen board of tiles: the time, the next zman, the halachic hour, the Hebrew date and more. Layouts switch by themselves for Shabbos, Yom Tov and times of day, with a dim night mode and a choice of themes, upright or sideways. Good for an old phone or tablet on the counter.

More widgets

Build your own widget from the rows you pick, a halachic hours clock, and a meat and milk widget.

App colors

Ocean, Forest, Sunrise, Plum, Parchment and Slate, each in light and dark. Wallpaper colors and Shemesh’s navy and gold stay free.

Alarms for another place

Work an alarm out for somewhere else, like a call before candle lighting in Yerushalayim, while Shabbos still follows where you are.

Meat and milk timer, work calendar

Start the timer when you finish meat and see when you can have milk, by your minhag’s waiting time. The work calendar lists when you leave early on Fridays and erev Yom Tov, and the Yom Tov days you need off, ready to send to your employer.

For shuls

Run the shul’s times from your phone

For a gabbai, a rav or whoever puts together the weekly schedule. The minyan schedule and the screen are part of Shemesh Pro.

Minyan schedule Pro

Set each minyan once as a rule, like “Mincha 15 minutes before sunset, rounded down to 5” or “Shacharis at 7:00”. Shemesh works out the times for one to four weeks, names each day’s holiday or parsha, and shares them as a table picture for the shul’s WhatsApp group, or as text.

Special days

A minyan can be only on, or never on, fast days, Rosh Chodesh, erev Shabbos, Chol HaMoed, Yom Tov, Elul or Selichos days, or only part of the year. For example, a later Shacharis on Rosh Chodesh, or Mincha moving after Yom Kippur.

The shul’s own place

The times follow the shul, not wherever your phone is. Pick its city, stand in the shul and use where you are, or paste its coordinates. Your own shitos still apply.

Zmanim on a screen Pro

Put the dashboard on a tablet in shul: the time, today’s zmanim, the next zman and the Hebrew date, switching by itself for Shabbos and Yom Tov.

Help

If something goes wrong

An alarm didn’t ring

Run Settings › Alarm check first. Then open Settings › Alarm history and tap Send report, so we can see what happened.

A time doesn’t match my luach

Open the zman and tap Compare with my luach to find which opinion your calendar uses. Still off? Email us the date, place and time you expected.

Updating to a new version

Beta updates arrive through Google Play like any other app update. Your alarms and settings are kept.
